Bullet Hell Chess

No turns. Every piece has a cooldown, and both armies move at the same time.

  • Tap a piece, then a square, or drag it. A full ring means it's ready.
  • Tap a cooling piece to queue a move. It fires the moment the ring fills.
  • Red arrows show enemy moves winding up. Dodge before the reticle closes.
  • Take their king to clear the wave. Lose yours and the run ends.
  • From wave 2 the board fights back. Every third wave is a boss. Clear wave 15 to win, then go endless.
  • Catch golden pieces before they escape for 5× points. Press P to pause.
